# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People watching native or embedded video on websites that do not offer a useful in-tab viewing mode.
They want the video as large as possible while retaining browser tabs, toolbars, and access to other applications.

## Product Purpose

Big Video detects website video players and fits the selected player inside the browser viewport without cropping or changing its original aspect ratio.
Success means the correct player is easy to expand, reliable to restore, and unobtrusive when unused.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative glow, emoji-heavy controls, intrusive overlays, video cropping, unexpected fullscreen transitions, and controls that compete with the website player.

## Design Principles

- Keep the video primary.
- Prefer predictable browser conventions.
- Offer a manual path when automatic detection cannot be certain.
- Restore the page exactly as it was.
- Explain unavailable behavior instead of failing silently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for the extension popup and injected controls.
Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, and descriptive accessible names.
